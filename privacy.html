Privacy & Support

Effective 8 October 2026. Lumen Deep is made by Adrien Buffin, an independent developer on the Gold Coast, Australia.

Privacy policy

0 dataLumen Deep does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (gold, upgrades, trophies, best scores and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this progress.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules up to two local reminders on your phone, such as a dive of the day. They are created on the device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off in the game options or in iOS Settings › Notifications › Lumen Deep.

Vibration

The game uses your phone's haptics for hits and explosions. You can switch this off with the Shake option in the game.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not use the internet to play. Apple may collect information under its own policies when you download the app from the App Store.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

My progress disappeared

Progress lives only on your phone. It is lost if the app is deleted or the phone is reset without a backup.

I don't want reminders

Turn reminders off in the game options, or in iOS Settings › Notifications › Lumen Deep.

The game feels slow

Close other apps and turn off Low Power Mode.
